Hu Jianmin: Source and Archive Directory

32 annotated resource records linking to original publications, student registers, European institutional archives, Gille manuscripts and Qiang-studies reception.

Scope / 使用说明： A = original pages examined; B = a contemporary publication or institutional catalogue locator; C = retrospective or secondary account; N = a negative finding only within the named source. Public catalogue access does not guarantee that the original is digitized. No Strasbourg diploma or original French thesis has been recovered. All outward links are public institutional or publication resources; private research files are not reproduced. 资料状态依据既有核查记录，外链没有在本次发布中全部逐一重新测试。
