Luzhou in Foreign-Language and Overseas Archives

A bilingual guide to original publications, public archive catalogues, travel texts, photographs, rural surveys and wartime material relating to Luzhou, Sichuan, from early nineteenth-century cartography through 1940.

01–07 · Cartography and travel

01 地名异写与地理消歧 / Historical spellings and geographic disambiguation

全时段 · 类型 / Type: 检索规范表 / authority and retrieval vocabulary · 状态 / Status: C（形式有文献依据；具体个别地名另见 P/Q）

四川泸州在英国旅行、传教和加拿大档案中经常写作 Luchow、Lu-chow、Luchow, Szechwan；1861年 Blakiston 使用更少见的 Lu (chow) 和 Town of Lu；法国传教和军事文献常写 Lou-tcheou、Lou-Tchéou、Loutchéou；民国时期英文报道还使用 Luhsien、Lu-hsien 和 LUHSIEN (LUCHOW)。宗教据点检索还需覆盖 Siao-shi、Siaoshi、Shao-shi；道路、交通及周边区域需同时搜索 Suifu / Sou Tcheou Fou、Chungking / Tchoung King Fou、Nachi、Kiangan。与安徽庐州府同形的 Luchow-foo / Lu-chou-fu 必须配合 Sichuan、Upper Yangtze、Suifu 或 Chungking 等独立地理锚点。

Productive forms include the British and Canadian Luchow, Lu-chow, Luchow, Szechwan; Blakiston's unusual Lu (chow) / Town of Lu; French Lou-tcheou, Lou-Tchéou, Loutchéou; and Republican-era Luhsien, Lu-hsien, LUHSIEN (LUCHOW). Mission searches should include outstation forms Siao-shi / Siaoshi / Shao-shi, alongside adjacent route nodes such as Suifu, Chungking, Nachi and Kiangan. Unanchored Luchow-foo often refers to the former Luzhou/Lu-chou prefecture in Anhui, centred on present-day Hefei, and must not be silently assigned to Sichuan.

02 Klaproth 的泸州分幅手绘地图 / Julius von Klaproth's manuscript Luzhou sheet

1831年前 / before 1831 · 类型 / Type: 手稿地图 / manuscript cartography · 状态 / Status: C 著录，原图待读 / catalogue identified, sheet awaiting inspection

法国国家图书馆收藏 Klaproth 的四川省22幅彩色描摹图，题为 Vingt deux feuilles de la province de Szu-tchhouan；第14幅的目录题名直接写作 Province de Szu-tchhouan. Nr. 14 : Lou-tcheou。这是将泸州单独作为分幅组织单位的早期欧洲手绘地图线索。馆藏 Collection Klaproth 0173-11，索书号 GE DD-2816 (11, 1-22 RES)，数字对象 IFN-55004874，BnF 记录 cb42046690g。地图底本是否为某一类清代中文地图，还须直接看第14幅并查源流。

The BnF describes a 22-sheet coloured manuscript tracing set of Sichuan by Julius von Klaproth, dated before 1831. Its sheet 14 is catalogued specifically as Lou-tcheou, making it an early European cartographic witness in which Luzhou is a discrete mapping unit rather than a passing provincial label. The catalogue reference is Collection Klaproth 0173-11, shelfmark GE DD-2816 (11, 1-22 RES), digital identifier IFN-55004874. The original cartographic source underlying the Sichuan tracings remains to be established.

03 Blakiston 于1861年实地经过泸州 / Thomas W. Blakiston's visit, 13 May 1861

1861年5月13日；出版1862年 / visit 13 May 1861; published 1862 · 类型 / Type: 旅行观察 / firsthand travel narrative · 状态 / Status: C 文本层 / text-level

Thomas Wright Blakiston 在 Five Months on the Yang-Tsze 中将泸州写作 Lu (chow)，记录其位于长江与 Fu-sung river 汇流处，描述远处可见的七层塔、镇中旧塔、木材和商业活动，以及连续降雨后上涨的江水与被淹的烟草田。队伍在城对岸停留并搜集叛乱消息；官员起初对其身份存疑。作者从自己的观察角度声称看不到城墙，该陈述不能直接当作泸州当时没有城墙的证明。沿途苎麻、红花、蔗糖等作物记录亦须区别城郊与整个旅行路径。

In Five Months on the Yang-Tsze, Blakiston records Lu (chow) on 13 May 1861, at the mouth of the Fu-sung river. His account mentions a distant seven-storey pagoda, another old pagoda, timber and trade, high river levels after prolonged rain, inundated tobacco plots and official suspicion of the expedition. His statement that he could see no enclosing wall is a viewpoint-specific observation, not proof of the absence of fortifications. Agricultural details along the approach route should be kept distinct from observations within the city.

04 法国湄公河考察队1868年的经济地理记录 / Francis Garnier's 1868 economic-geographic account

1868年5月10日；出版1873年 / visit 10 May 1868; published 1873 · 类型 / Type: 考察报告 / expedition account · 状态 / Status: C 文本层；数字待核 / reported figures require checking

法国考察队由宜宾向汉口返程，1868年5月10日抵达 Lou-tcheou。Garnier 编纂的报告将泸州写成盐业转运枢纽：盐井及以天然燃料煎盐的技术、水路运盐、运往贵州及川滇南部的再分配、赤水河流域经纳溪输来的贵州木材、造船和沿江煤层，均在这一段建立联系。文本称平均每天约20艘船、约120吨盐运至泸州；这是考察队的当时陈述，统计形成过程仍须检验。

Garnier's published account of the 1866–68 French Mekong expedition records arrival at Lou-tcheou on 10 May 1868. It situates the city within salt redistribution, saline wells and fuel extraction, timber arriving from Guizhou via the Chishui/Naxi route, shipbuilding and coal extraction. The published approximation of twenty boats and 120 tonnes of salt daily is an expeditionary claim whose method of calculation is not yet established. This source provides an early economic-geographic baseline independent of later mission writing.

05 1895—1896年 Madrolle 手绘路线图 / Claudius Madrolle's itinerary map, 1895–96

1895–1896 · 类型 / Type: 手绘旅行路线图 / manuscript itinerary map · 状态 / Status: C 著录，路线待描 / catalogue-level

BnF 馆藏 Itinéraire de M. Cl. Madrolle du Tomkim au Tibet et en Chine, 1895-1896...，明确经过 Soui-fou, Lou-tchéou, Tchoung-kin。馆藏号 GE C-18175，数字对象 IFN-53195579，比例尺约1:1,000,000，描摹图约60×100厘米。它可与 Klaproth 的早期行政地理图、d'Amade 的军事踏勘材料形成不同时期的空间对读。

The BnF lists a large manuscript itinerary map by Claudius Madrolle, c. 1895–96, whose printed route description explicitly includes Suifu, Luzhou and Chongqing. Its shelfmark is GE C-18175; digital identifier IFN-53195579. It is potentially valuable for reconstructing late-nineteenth-century route topology in conjunction with Klaproth's older cartography and d'Amade's reconnaissance.

06 Isabella Bird 1897年访问与泸州宝塔照片 / Isabella Bird's 1897 visit and the pagoda photograph

1897年旅行；1899年出版 / 1897 visit; 1899 publication · 类型 / Type: 旅行文字、原始摄影 / travel account and photograph · 状态 / Status: C 出版物与图片著录 / published source identified

Isabella L. Bird（婚后常署 Bishop）1897年经过泸州，1899年书中有 Pagoda near Luchow 图版，并记载当地鸦片吸食相关的官员说法。她在前言中说明，除明确注明的例外外，书中插图据自己的照片制作。该图与书在书格已有重合，但原书前言、图片来源、文字对应和地域判定仍具有独立的史料价值；其听闻的鸦片数字属于官员转述，不能与1909年种植面积混成同一统计。

Bird/Bishop's 1897 journey and 1899 book provide the plate Pagoda near Luchow and a description of local opium use partly mediated by officials. Her preface attributes illustrations generally to her photographs, subject to named exceptions. Existing online reproduction is useful for comparison, but original edition, plate placement and photographic provenance remain part of the evidence. Statements about consumption should not be equated with later figures on poppy cultivation.

07 Archibald John Little 1901年的城市与商品观察 / Archibald John Little's account of urban and material life

1901年出版 / published 1901 · 类型 / Type: 旅行记、城市观察 / travel and urban observation · 状态 / Status: C 文本层；旧城位置 Q / text-level; old town Q

Mount Omi and Beyond 中的泸州段将城市放在沱江与长江交汇处，涉及城市街道、商铺、临水寺庙、带花园的民居、内地会租用的住宅、盐务官署及仓库、军营和盐船换船员的日常运输细节。作者特别提到市面上的红玉髓项链和角梳，还写到一处位于高地的旧泸州遗址；后者的确切位置和与考古文献中 Old Luchow 的关系仍待检验。

Little's Mount Omi and Beyond preserves unusually dense observations of city streets, retail goods, houses and gardens, temples, CIM premises, Salt Gabelle offices, military facilities and the practice of changing crews on salt junks. His references to cornelian necklaces, horn combs and an abandoned older Luchow site offer leads for material-culture and historical-topography research. Neither the old-town location nor its equivalence with a later archaeological label is established.

08–14 · French mission and military

08 Gourdin 与1864年起的泸州常驻传教档案 / François Gourdin and the resident MEP record

1864–1872；1896–约1905 / 1864–72; 1896–c.1905 · 类型 / Type: 传教档案及通信 / mission personnel and correspondence · 状态 / Status: C 人员年表 / personnel chronology

MEP 的 François Gourdin 于1864年底以泸州为主要驻地，1872年离开；1896年再度返回，在泸州约工作九年。两段较长的驻地经历可作为搜索法国私人信件、年度报告、方言例句和地方人际网络的时间支架。注意 Gourdin 的区域性语言著作不能在未经原文定位的情况下逐项归为泸州口语证据。

IRFA's record places Gourdin at Lou-tcheou as his principal base from late 1864 until 1872, and again from 1896 for roughly nine years. His long local connection supplies a chronological anchor for recovering private letters, annual mission records, linguistic examples and interactions with local people. His general Western Mandarin publications are not automatically Luzhou dialect records.

09 Julien Clerc《在中国十六年》书信集 / Julien Clerc's Seize ans en Chine

1872–1885驻地；1887年出版 / residence 1872–85; published 1887 · 类型 / Type: 身后编辑出版的信件集 / posthumously edited letters · 状态 / Status: C 馆藏与数字版；具体页待精读 / catalogue and digital text located

Clerc 负责泸州教区十三年，1887年由其外甥 Jules Viard 编刊的 Seize ans en Chine 共前置页 viii + 363页。它是考察1870—1880年代泸州学校、救济院、地方人士、宗教关系、日常生活、医疗、1876年冲突威胁及1885年疫病的重要长篇文本。其材料经过亲属身后编辑，书内信件日期与发表时编辑介入程度应分别记录。

Clerc administered the Lou-tcheou district for thirteen years. His nephew Jules Viard edited and published the viii + 363-page Seize ans en Chine in 1887. The collection is a substantial route to district-level observations on schools, hospice activity, local interlocutors, religious relations, everyday practice and the 1885 epidemic. It is a mediated posthumous publication rather than an untouched run of autograph correspondence.

10 1884年救济院、1885年疫病与当时讣闻 / Hospice, epidemic and contemporary necrology

1884–1885 · 类型 / Type: 机构记录、年报、讣闻 / institutional report and necrology · 状态 / Status: C 目录与后见记述；死亡数字待交叉验证 / catalogue and later notices; number uncorroborated

IRFA 传记记录 Clerc 于1884年创办救济院，1885年5月22日在泸州因病去世。其传记称当次伤寒疫情据说导致泸州逾万人死亡；这个数字应作为来源所述保留，而非人口统计事实。IRFA 指向当时的 C.-R. 1885, p. 202 讣闻，另有1885年度川南报告，二者的叙述层级、统计出处、院舍运作及中国医者的信息需要拆开校勘。

IRFA records Clerc's founding of a hospice in 1884 and his death in Lou-tcheou on 22 May 1885. Its later biography repeats a reported epidemic toll exceeding 10,000, which remains an attributed claim, not an independently established count. A contemporary necrology cited as C.-R. 1885, p. 202 and the separately located 1885 Southern Sichuan annual report should be examined independently, particularly for medical practice, local collaborators and the derivation of any mortality figure.

11 Scherrier 与1887年泸州城市大火线索 / Paul Scherrier and the reported 1887 city fire

1886–1890 · 类型 / Type: 传教年报、事后传记 / mission reports and later biography · 状态 / Status: C 传记叙述，独立火灾证据待取 / later notice confirmed, independent event evidence pending

IRFA 的 Paul Scherrier 传记称，1887年一场大火毁坏泸州的大部分区域，而教会建筑险些受毁。该描述构成城市空间与灾害史的高价值检索线索，但目前应注明“传记如此记载”，待查1887年或相邻年度的同期川南报告，以及中国地方志、城市修复文书、商贸与领事文件。IRFA 的1887年报已找到数字版入口，仍需核定其中是否确实载有火灾。

Scherrier's IRFA biography reports a major 1887 fire that destroyed much of Lou-tcheou and almost consumed the mission station. The event is an important urban-history lead, but its scale currently rests on a later biographical notice. The identified 1887 Southern Sichuan annual report and independent Chinese or consular sources must be checked before asserting the event's exact geography or losses.

12 1894—1895年川南年报与教产冲突 / Southern Sichuan annual reports and 1895 crisis

1894–1895 · 类型 / Type: 当时印行的年度报告 / contemporary annual reports · 状态 / Status: C 数字文件 / digitized reports located

川南1894年报告将 Scherrier 明确置于泸州；1895年报告提到在法方代表 Gérard 干预下泸州府的教会建筑得以保全，并记载其他受波及区域的札记、账簿与名册损失。既可用于重建教会网络与地方冲突，也提示研究者：档案失存本身可能由历史事件造成，不能简单把现存档案密度看作历史活动密度。

The digitized 1894 Southern Sichuan report places Scherrier in Lou-tcheou, while the 1895 report describes the preservation of mission property in the prefecture following intervention by the French representative Gérard and reports the loss of records elsewhere in the affected region. These annual reports supply both event evidence and an important reminder that archival survival was historically contingent.

13 Gourdin 及川南传教语言出版物 / Western Mandarin lexicons and the MEP print network

1893–1906，续版至1930年代 / 1893–1906, later editions · 类型 / Type: 词典、语言手册、印刷史 / dictionaries, manuals and print history · 状态 / Status: C 书目；具体泸州例句 Q / works identified, locality of examples unknown

Gourdin 参与1893年《华西官话汉法词典》，并著有1895/1896年 Premières études de la langue mandarine parlée 等作品。Joseph de Gaztelu 的1900年法汉小词典和1906年汉法词汇表则应归入同一川南—香港纳匝肋印书网络。重要校订： 一份早期专题笔记曾误说 Gaztelu 临时主持泸州教区；较晚的地图专题包据 IRFA 年表订正为其在 Clerc 临终时在场，其后接管的是江安（Kiang-Gan）教区，而非泸州。因而 Gaztelu 语言著作的泸州关联只能作为区域网络线索，不能据此认定词条具有泸州当地采样来源。

Gourdin's work connects Lou-tcheou to the 1893 Western-China Mandarin dictionary and later language manuals. Gaztelu's dictionaries (1900; 1906) belong to the wider Southern Sichuan and Hong Kong Nazareth print network. Correction: an earlier note wrongly assigned Gaztelu temporary administration of Lou-tcheou. A correction based on IRFA chronology, identifies Kiang-Gan as the district he took over. His lexicons therefore offer regional comparison, not demonstrated Luzhou field data.

14 Albert d'Amade 的泸州—宜宾军事地理踏勘 / Albert d'Amade's photographic-cartographic reconnaissance

1888–1890 · 类型 / Type: 军事地理照片、底片、手绘地图 / military reconnaissance, negatives and manuscript maps · 状态 / Status: C 著录；地点精确定位 Q / archival record located; precise bridge location unresolved

法军武官 d'Amade 的 ECPAD 馆藏分为图册 D137-11 与底片 D137-12；其中 D137-12-52 原标题指向从 Lou Tcheou 至 Sou Tcheou Fou 道路上的 Koung Pou Tçiao 桥梁。SHD 的 GR 2 K 254 有相关图册与行程地图，GR 10H 13, dossier 4 更有照片对页小幅手绘地图的线索。相邻底片记录重庆、长江沿岸、宜宾、船只和其他交通节点，可用于恢复一段连续的道路与水运观察，而桥的现今准确位置仍待核实。另有 ANOM GGI 24713 和 GGI 6632 的交通商业技术报告线索。

ECPAD identifies d'Amade's album D137-11 and negative series D137-12; negative D137-12-52 depicts the bridge of Koung Pou Tçiao on the road from Lou Tcheou to Sou Tcheou Fou. Related SHD holdings include GR 2 K 254 and GR 10H 13, dossier 4, the latter described as captioned photographs with facing small manuscript maps. Adjacent images and ANOM commercial-access-route reports position the photograph within a larger intelligence corpus. The bridge has not yet been geolocated precisely.

15 内地会泸州站与 SOAS 图片类目 / CIM Luchow station and the SOAS photo handlist

1890年设站；主要材料19世纪末至20世纪初 / station established 1890 · 类型 / Type: 差会目录、照片手册 / station directories and photo inventory · 状态 / Status: C 目录层；单张照片待识别 / catalogue-level

1918、1919年的差会目录将内地会 Luchow 站的起始时间列为1890年。SOAS 所藏 CIM/OMF 照片超过7,000张，Szechwan and Sikang 类目按景观、寺庙、建筑、交通、就业、农业、人物等分组，手册中直搜 Luchow、Lu-chow、Siaoshi 未发现地点标题命中。这反映手册的省级分类粒度，不能据此推断没有泸州照片。可结合 Tom James、W. T. Herbert、Fred Bird、J. E. Blick、R. Cunningham、A. H. Barnham 等人员姓名检索。

Directories from 1918–19 date the CIM Luchow station to 1890. SOAS's CIM/OMF photographic handlist describes more than 7,000 photographs, with Sichuan subject groups covering landscape, shrines, buildings, transport, occupations and agriculture. Exact place-name searches in the handlist did not return Luchow, Lu-chow or Siaoshi; this is a limitation of catalogue granularity rather than proof that Luchow images are absent. Person-name searches and adjacent provincial subject folders remain necessary.

16 加拿大循道宗泸州站及 Jolliffe 罕见誊写回忆录 / Canadian Methodist station and Jolliffe's typescript

1908年设站；约1910年代叙事 / station established 1908; early twentieth-century recollections · 类型 / Type: 家族档案、约99页油印或誊写回忆录 / family fonds and c.99-page typescript · 状态 / Status: C 著录，需全文提取 / located; full extraction pending

Victoria University 的资料将加拿大循道宗泸州站始建于1908年。C. J. P. 与 Gertrude Jolliffe 的 Our Work in China 保存在加拿大联合教会档案 F3309，数字项目标识 2016.074C/TR，现见副本约99页。它包含辅助营建的中国合作者 Hsu Hwan Tin、为在世母亲预制棺木的叙事、川西南惯用说法、敲钟计时、轿行、村镇及医疗回忆。这些都是从地方实践进入原始语言材料的线索，但回忆录中的“孝道”等解释属于作者阐释，须与中国来源分开。

Victoria University places the Canadian Methodist Luchow station's foundation in 1908. The Jolliffes' approximately 99-page Our Work in China, associated with United Church of Canada fonds F3309 and digital item 2016.074C/TR, contains retrospective local notes on Chinese building collaborators, coffins and filial practice, local expressions, timekeeping, sedan-chair travel, outstations and medical recollections. This unusually granular typescript is a high-priority social-history source, but its descriptions of custom are the memoirist's interpretations.

36–41 · Rural survey and wartime

36 1931年石马场农事集会 / The 1931 Shi Ma Chang rural-extension meeting

1931年秋巡行；1932年3月发表 / autumn 1931; printed March 1932 · 类型 / Type: 农村教育巡行报道 / rural-extension itinerary · 状态 / Status: C 原刊；村镇现代对应 P / printed report; modern identification provisional

R. S. Longley 在1932年3月 West China Missionary News 发表前一年行程，列出泸州城区与 Shi Ma Chang。当地绅士在赶场日于广场组织农民大会，Li Min Liang 发言，介绍《良种选择》《害虫》等农业印刷品，并在条件允许时加入公共卫生讲授。这是将传教机构活动、地方绅士组织、场镇空间与农业知识流通放在同一事件中的同期材料。

Longley's March 1932 report describes a 1931 rural-extension itinerary in which Luchow City and Shi Ma Chang are explicit stops. On market day local gentry convened a farmers' meeting in the town square; Li Min Liang spoke, with seed-selection and pest-control materials and, where medical personnel were available, public-health sessions. The event provides a rare junction between mission networks, market-town civic organization and agricultural extension.

37 Price 1936—1937年73处农村教会调查中的石马场 / Shihmachang in Frank Wilson Price's 1936–37 sample survey

1936–1937调查；1941年首印；1948年第二版 / field survey 1936–37; first printed 1941; second edition 1948 · 类型 / Type: 农村社会调查与原始问卷线索 / rural sample survey and schedule lead · 状态 / Status: C 出版条目；原表和地图待寻 / publication verified; original schedule unresolved

Frank Wilson Price 的 The Rural Church in China: A Survey 所列73处个案中，第27项为 Shihmachang, Szechwan，调查人 Rev. A. C. Hoffman，通讯地点 Luchow, Sze.。其调查在1936—1937年进行，1938年完成耶鲁博士论文／书稿，1941年上海 Kelly & Walsh 已印成的初版因同年12月局势未能正常流通，1948年版为第二版且基本是首个广泛流通版。调查法要求县图、社区图、教区图或村镇图，有些表格附照片；当前无法证明第27项实际附有哪一种图像或附件。

Price's 73-case national survey identifies case no.27 as Shihmachang, Szechwan, surveyed by Rev. A. C. Hoffman of Luchow, Sze.. Fieldwork occurred in 1936–37, the dissertation/manuscript was completed in 1938, the first edition printed in Shanghai in 1941 did not circulate normally, and the 1948 edition is the second. Maps and sometimes photographs formed part of the survey method, but no particular attachment can yet be assigned to case 27 without recovering the original packet.

38 1939年四川教会全省调查及约1944年复查线索 / The 1939 Szechwan-wide church survey and later comparison

1939；约1944年复查线索 / 1939; possible c.1944 follow-up · 类型 / Type: 全省机构调查 / province-wide institutional survey · 状态 / Status: C Price 所刊统计；原始县级记录待寻 / published figures, underlying returns missing

Price 1948年版另有 Survey of Szechwan Churches 章节，称南京神学院于1939年调查全省499个城乡教会、13,653名新教会友，并给出省际比例和城乡分布的分析。它与1936—1937年跨13省、73处个案的抽样调查是两套不同调查。Price 还说“五年后”另一项调查显示会友人数增长约30%，可作为约1944年复查线索。上述数字目前确证为 Price 文本所刊数据，尚非依据原始问卷重新计算的结果，更不能把全省数据直接当作泸州地方统计。

Price's 1948 edition separately reports a 1939 Nanking Theological Seminary survey of 499 urban and rural churches and 13,653 Protestant members in Szechwan. It must be distinguished from the 1936–37 national 73-case study. A reported survey five years later may point to c.1944 comparative returns. These are confirmed figures in Price's publication, not independently reconstructed counts, and the published province totals do not furnish Luchow-specific denominators or observations.

40 Gerald S. Bell 1939年10月对毁坏区的报道 / Gerald S. Bell's October 1939 damage account

1939年10月 West China Missionary News p.405 的 FROM LUCHOW 是 Gerald S. Bell 信件节录，描述医院与妇女传教会建筑焚毁、学校、住宅和教堂受损，提到西门、两江交汇处、Hoffman 住所与 No.2 house，并给出部分炸弹和人员伤亡的目测估计。因其为经过编刊的信件节录，完整原信以及机构报告、中文灾害记录和 UCC 地图是重建损毁范围的必要参照。

Its bomb and casualty estimates should remain attributed to the letter rather than converted into verified totals. 41 Hoffman 1940年泸州救济委员会自述 / A. C. Hoffmann's 1940 relief-committee report

1940年6月发表 / published June 1940 · 类型 / Type: 救济机构自述 / relief-committee self-report · 状态 / Status: C 原刊；比较性评价需另证 / printed report; institutional judgements unverified

1940年6月 West China Missionary News pp.308–309 署名 A. C. Hoffmann（该期使用双 n），记录1939年轰炸后泸州教会及居民的消防、抢救、临时居所、食宿、衣被供应和医疗转移工作。报告描述教堂挤满流离者、地方机关调拨较大院落、供水受损、暑热及个别霍乱和痢疾病例，并称医院安全转移五十余名患者。它提供了较具体的救济后勤资料，但对教会成效的评价仍属机构自述。

Hoffmann's June 1940 WCMN article (pp.308–309; double n in this issue) describes fire-fighting, rescue, temporary shelter, food, bedding, disrupted water supply, disease risk and the transfer of more than fifty hospital patients after the bombing. It is a detailed relief-logistics witness, but comparative claims about institutional performance and beneficiary groups belong to the relief committee's own account and need outside corroboration.
